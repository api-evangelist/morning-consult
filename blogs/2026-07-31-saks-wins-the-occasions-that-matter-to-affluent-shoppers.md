The bottom line up front Saks Fifth Avenue over-indexes on four of the occasions a luxury retailer should own: self-reward (Mental Advantage +8.1), weddings (+6.0), special-event outfits (+5.9), and browsing to discover new styles (+5.7), each above the +5 mark that signals genuine strength. Saks performs well above its size on exactly the discretionary, aspiration-driven occasions that matter to affluent shoppers.
