THE BOTTOM LINE The brand winning the battle for daters’ attention isn’t a standalone dating app — it’s a free feature built into Facebook. Facebook Dating leads 22 of the 25 occasions measured among the category’s major brands. Tinder leads two, even though far fewer people have heard of Facebook Dating than of Tinder, the best-known name in the category.
