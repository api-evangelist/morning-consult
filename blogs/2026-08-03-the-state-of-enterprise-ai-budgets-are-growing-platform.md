In order to understand the current state of AI adoption at the enterprise level, Morning Consult conducted a survey of 3,003 U.S. Business Decision Makers (BDMs) — employees who are influencers or primary decision-makers for strategic initiatives, operations, resources, or other business decisions, holding a manager-level title or above. This survey was conducted July 21–24, 2026.
